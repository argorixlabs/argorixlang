# ESP-012 — Resolution, types and modules in Argorix

Status: IN PROGRESS
Lane: Claude.
Dependencies: ESP-011 (PR #54).

## Observable objective

Move Core's semantic decisions out of Rust. That covers scopes, signatures,
calls, recursion, types, exhaustiveness, effects and the import graph, with
permitted and forbidden cycles resolved deterministically. Paths stay inside
the package, and compilation permissions are declared.

## Acceptance criteria (Plan Espada)

- [ ] It validates its own sources.
- [ ] It detects duplicate symbols, wrong types, missing returns, misused
      handles and invalid imports.
- [ ] Its error decisions have independent tests.

## ESP-012.A

- `compiler/check.argx` is the checker.
  - Types are interned in a table, so two types are equal exactly when their
    positions are.
  - Declarations and their members live in flat buffers. Names are source
    tokens, compared by text.
  - Scopes are a stack of bindings with a depth.
  - The checker follows the stage0 rules one by one, including where each
    diagnostic points.
- To give the checker what it reads, the parser now tags each node with:
  - its kind;
  - its name token;
  - the last token of its path;
  - a value: a literal, an operator or a length;
  - a flag: `pub`, `mut`, a guarded arm, or a suffix.
- `argorixc core-check-dump` prints the stage0 diagnostics in the same form
  (`spec/core/check.md`).
- **Stage0 fix:** two parts of the stage0 checker followed the iteration order
  of a hash map.
  - The order of `InfiniteType` diagnostics could change between runs; they are
    now reported in program order.
  - The lists of missing variants and fields in messages are now sorted.

**Evidence.** `crates/argorixc/tests/check_differential.rs` runs
`tests/selfhost/check/check_files.argx` over 140 files through the
compiler-host boundary and compares each dump byte for byte with stage0's,
without ESP-012.B's codes. The files are:

- the compiler, which includes the checker's own source;
- the standard library;
- every fixture corpus and the spec corpus;
- the lexer, parser and checker samples. The checker samples cover
  declarations, expressions and statements.

All 140 agree. Moving one diagnostic by one byte fails exactly the files where
it fires. `tests/selfhost/check/cases.json` runs `check_files` under gcc and
clang, with and without sanitizers.

## Scope

A checker in Argorix, still built and run through the stage0 frontend and the
C backend. It does not lower or emit code (ESP-013).
